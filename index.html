About the Guild

LootTheBox is a retail and entertainment corporation that sells mystery boxes blending physical collectibles, such as scannable keychains and pins, with digital in-game rewards. Owned by shareholders and overseen by a board of directors, the company utilizes a structured leadership team to ensure strategic planning and responsible decision-making. This corporate framework promotes operational efficiency and scalability, allowing the business to raise capital and maintain long-term continuity beyond individual ownership.
